Soft dimensional illustrated style, smooth gently rounded surfaces and subtle highlights, matching the existing friendly coral-orange heart brand. Actual transparent alpha background, no backdrop, no white square, no checkerboard, no text, no letters, no watermark, no extra decorative symbols, no photorealistic skin, no realistic anatomy complexity. High-quality clean edges, friendly and dignified rather than distressed.
